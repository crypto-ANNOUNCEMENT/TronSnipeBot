// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TronSnipeBot — the #1 TRON meme coin sniper.

What is TronSnipeBot and how does it work?+
TronSnipeBot is an automated trading platform built specifically for the TRON blockchain. It continuously monitors SunPump and other TRON-based DEXes for newly launched meme coins, analyzes them in real time using AI, and executes buy orders in under 200ms when your configured criteria are met. It connects to your TronLink or WalletConnect wallet via signature authentication only — your private keys never leave your device.
Is my wallet and funds safe with TronSnipeBot?+
Yes. TronSnipeBot uses a fully non-custodial, signature-only model. We never request your seed phrase or private keys. All transactions are constructed in the browser, signed by your wallet, and broadcast directly to the TRON network. We never hold your funds at any point. Our architecture has been reviewed by independent security professionals and smart contract auditors.
Which wallets does TronSnipeBot support?+
We currently support TronLink (browser extension and mobile app) and WalletConnect-compatible TRON wallets. TronLink is recommended for the best performance due to its native TRON integration and lowest latency. Support for additional TRON-compatible wallets is on our roadmap for Q3 2026.
How fast is the sniper? What is the execution speed?+
Our median execution time is 182ms from token detection to confirmed transaction on-chain. This is achieved through our proprietary multi-region RPC infrastructure with load balancing across premium TRON full nodes. Pro and Institutional plan users get dedicated RPC endpoints for even lower latency in high-traffic launch moments.
What is the AI rug detection accuracy rate?+
Our AI rug detection model achieves 99.1% accuracy validated against historical TRON token launches from 2023–2025. The model analyzes smart contract bytecode, developer wallet history, liquidity lock status, token distribution patterns, honeypot indicators, and over 40 other on-chain signals simultaneously in real time before any snipe is executed.
Does TronSnipeBot work with SunPump specifically?+
Yes — SunPump is our primary deep integration. We maintain a direct connection to SunPump's contract factory and monitor every new token launch event in real time. The scanner is optimized for the SunPump bonding curve model and automatically detects graduation events, liquidity migrations, and key market cap milestones.
What is the difference between Quick Snipe, Auto Buy, Instant Sell, and Panic Exit?+
Quick Snipe executes a single buy at your current configured parameters immediately. Auto Buy sets a standing order to buy automatically when a new qualifying token is detected. Instant Sell closes your entire position at market price in one click. Panic Exit is an emergency function that sells all open positions simultaneously using maximum slippage to guarantee exit regardless of current market conditions — use only when needed.
How does the whale tracker work?+
We maintain a live curated database of the top 500 TRON whale wallets identified by historical trading volume, profitable trade history, and community tagging. Every transaction from these wallets is monitored in real time via on-chain events. When a whale enters a new token, you receive an instant alert with the contract address, buy amount, entry price, and a direct snipe link.
Can I use TronSnipeBot on mobile devices?+
Yes. The platform is fully responsive and thoroughly tested on iOS and Android. You can use it via the TronLink mobile app's built-in browser or any mobile browser with WalletConnect support. The mobile experience includes all core sniper features, live token feed, real-time notifications, and the complete DeFi widget panel.
What fees does TronSnipeBot charge beyond the subscription?+
The platform charges a 0.5% performance fee on profitable snipes only — zero fee on losing trades. Standard TRON network transaction fees (energy and bandwidth) apply as normal. For Borrow and Credit features, specific protocol fees are clearly displayed before you confirm any transaction. There are absolutely no hidden charges or surprise costs.
How do Telegram signal integrations work?+
You connect TronSnipeBot to your Telegram account and specify alpha channels or group chats to monitor. Our NLP parser extracts token contract addresses from messages automatically and can trigger snipes when a signal is detected. You control position sizing, slippage tolerance, and max-per-signal limits to manage risk. Available on Pro and Institutional plans.
What is the on-chain credit system and who qualifies?+
The Credit module generates a dynamic credit score based on your on-chain TRON trading history, wallet age, total volume, and repayment history on our protocol. Higher scores unlock larger credit lines up to $50,000 USDT equivalent at lower rates. Credit lines are collateral-free for verified high-score users, and all terms are enforced by audited smart contracts on TRON.
How does the Borrow feature work in TronSnipeBot?+
Borrow allows you to deposit TRX, USDT TRC-20, or other supported TRON assets as collateral and borrow USDT or TRX against them at a configurable LTV ratio up to 80%. Borrowed funds can be immediately deployed for additional snipes, staking, or liquidity provision. Liquidation occurs automatically via smart contract if your health factor drops below 1.0 to protect the protocol.
What APY rates does the staking vault offer?+
Staking APYs range from 12% (Bronze, 30-day lock) to 68% (Diamond, 365-day lock). Rates are variable and updated weekly based on protocol revenue. Diamond tier stakers also receive a share of platform trading fees in addition to base staking rewards, making it the highest-yield option for committed long-term TRX holders who believe in the ecosystem.
Is TronSnipeBot available globally without restrictions?+
TronSnipeBot is technically accessible globally. However, it is your responsibility to ensure that using automated crypto trading tools complies with the laws and regulations in your specific jurisdiction. Users in certain regions may be subject to local restrictions on DeFi services or automated trading. We recommend consulting a legal professional in your country before using the platform.
How do I set up stop loss and take profit automation?+
In the Sniper Panel, enter your desired Take Profit % (e.g., 200 for 2x) and Stop Loss % (e.g., 30 to exit if price drops 30% from entry). When Auto Sell is toggled on, the bot monitors your open positions and triggers sells automatically when either level is hit. The Trailing Stop feature dynamically adjusts your stop loss upward as the price rises, locking in gains while letting winners run further.
What data sources does the Live Token Scanner use?+
The scanner aggregates data from SunPump contract factory events, TRON on-chain data via dedicated full nodes, DEX liquidity pool data from SunSwap and JustSwap, holder data from TronScan, social volume from X/Twitter and Telegram APIs, and our proprietary developer wallet blacklist database which is updated in real time based on on-chain behavior.
Do you offer a developer API for programmatic access?+
Yes — the Institutional plan includes full REST and WebSocket API access with no rate limits. Developers can access the token scanner feed, AI risk scores, whale activity events, and execute snipes programmatically. API documentation is available at docs.tron-snipe-bot.vercel.app. A sandboxed test environment is provided for development and integration testing.
Can I run multiple sniper configurations simultaneously?+
Pro plan allows up to 5 simultaneous sniper configurations running in parallel, each with independent contract targets, position sizes, and individual risk parameters. Institutional plan supports unlimited parallel sniper instances and team member seats, allowing multiple traders to operate from a shared dashboard with role-based permissions and granular access controls.
What support options are available to users?+
Free plan users have access to our community Discord and comprehensive documentation. Pro plan users get 24/7 live chat support with average response times under 5 minutes. Institutional clients receive a dedicated account manager, a private Telegram support channel, and direct access to our engineering team for custom integrations and any technical escalations.
How does MEV and front-run protection work on TRON?+
TronSnipeBot routes your transactions through our private relay infrastructure so they are invisible to other bots monitoring the public TRON mempool. We also use transaction obfuscation techniques and dynamic gas priority bidding to ensure your snipes land before competing bots can detect and copy them. This MEV protection is active by default for all subscription tiers at no extra cost.
What liquidity pools are available in the Pool section?+
The Pool section currently features six curated TRON liquidity pools: TRX/USDT (18.4% APY, $4.2M TVL), SUN/TRX (34.7% APY), USDT/USDC stable pool (6.2% APY), BTT/TRX (42.1% APY), WIN/TRX (56.8% APY), and JST/TRX (28.3% APY). New pools are added regularly based on community voting and liquidity depth. All pools are powered by SunSwap's audited smart contracts.
How does TronSnipeBot handle failed transactions?+
When a transaction fails due to slippage, network congestion, or other issues, TronSnipeBot automatically retries via an alternative RPC node with adjusted parameters. You can configure a retry count (1–5 attempts) and timeout window in settings. Failed snipes are logged in your history with the exact error code and reason, allowing you to diagnose patterns and optimize your configuration for future launches.
Can I import or export my sniper settings and PnL history?+
Yes. All sniper configurations can be exported as JSON files and re-imported on any device or browser, making it easy to migrate or share setups with team members. PnL history is exportable as CSV for tax reporting or personal analysis. Pro users also get access to a full analytics dashboard with charts showing win rate, average ROI, and token performance breakdowns by category and time period.
What blockchains beyond TRON are supported for cross-chain swaps?+
Cross-chain swap support currently includes TRON (primary), Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Solana, Arbitrum, and Optimism — with more than 20 chains in total accessible through our aggregator bridge. All cross-chain swaps are routed through audited bridging protocols to minimize counterparty risk. TRON-native swaps via SunSwap offer the lowest fees and fastest settlement at under 3 seconds confirmation time.
